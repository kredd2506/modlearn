Was it the chip or the software?

Started September 2026 · in progress

A 2025 UCSD study (arXiv:2507.00418) served 12 LLMs on the Qualcomm Cloud AI 100 Ultra and on 4×/8× NVIDIA A100, using vLLM on both. Its headline was 10–35× lower power. Per token, though, Qualcomm was more efficient on only 3 of 12 models, and it served most models at ~6–25 tok/s.

Since then, Qualcomm acquired Modular, and ModCon 2026 showed MAX running on Cloud AI 100 Ultra. This project re-runs the study with MAX as the serving engine to test whether those numbers reflected the chip or the software.

Hypotheses, stated before any runs:

  • H1: on Qualcomm, MAX substantially outperforms the paper's vLLM numbers
  • H2: on the A100, MAX matches or beats vLLM in throughput and tok/s/W
  • H3: a fair A100 setup (1 GPU for ≤8B models) shrinks Qualcomm's apparent advantage
  • H4: one Mojo kernel runs unchanged on Apple, NVIDIA, and Qualcomm silicon
